# POINTX × Dynamic Yield — Presenter Cheat Sheet

**One-line pitch:** *Dynamic Yield turns POINTX's static shelves into a 1:1 experience — ranking every shelf by what the member's balance can reach and what they actually like, hiding what they can't use or already have, promoting the right way to earn, and letting members ask for what they want through a Shopping Muse assistant that answers from the same catalogue and their live balance.*

**Controls:** `P` presenter panel · `T` flip "POINTX today" vs DY · banner variant **A/B/C** · **Why this?** / **Why this order?** opens the reasoning rail with the sorting-optimiser board · **SCB button** opens scan & pay.

**Run order:** Mint first (cold start → real-time → first earn), then Korn (expiry, suppression, Wealth, big-ticket top-up), then the **live persona switch** and the **`T` comparison** for the close.

---
